GBV Support Resources

GBV

BOCONGO GBV Hotline

24/7 free support line for GBV survivors. Confidential counselling, referrals, and crisis response.

0800 600 111 — Free

Gaborone Magistrate Court

Apply for a Protection Order (PO) against an abuser. Free to apply, same-day emergency orders available. Visit the Clerk of Court at Gaborone Magistrate Court, Khama Crescent.

Stepping Stones International

Provides safe shelter, trauma counselling, legal aid, and reintegration support for GBV survivors and vulnerable youth in Botswana.

+267 313 3311

Botswana Police GBV Unit

Dedicated GBV response unit within the Botswana Police Service. Officers trained to handle domestic violence and sexual offences with sensitivity. Available at all major stations.

Call 999

Childline Botswana

24/7 free helpline for children in distress, abuse, or danger. Confidential support for minors and caregivers.

116 — Free for Minors
